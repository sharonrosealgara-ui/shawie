Our Story

Crafted with love, since 2016.

“We believe a celebration should feel like you — every flavor, every flower, every frame.”

SHAWIE Bakes & Events began with a single tiered cake and a deep love for bringing people together. Nine years on, we’re a full-service studio styling, baking and filming the moments that matter most — from intimate garden weddings to grand ballroom debuts.

Because cake, styling, flowers and film all live under one roof, your day flows as one cohesive story. No mismatched vendors, no missed details — just a team that knows your vision inside out.
