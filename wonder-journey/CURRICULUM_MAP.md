# 🗺️ Wonder Journey OS — Curriculum Map (World 1)

**World 1: Discover the Philippines** · **72 Adventures** · classes **Mon / Tue / Fri**
· **Mon, Jul 6, 2026 → Fri, Dec 18, 2026** (the last teaching week of December).

> **Step 1 of the curriculum system.** Every Unit, Adventure, scheduled date, learning objective,
> and the **Math**, **English**, **Bible**, and **character Value** focus that keeps academics building
> gradually and tied to the adventure. ✅ = full interactive content already built; 🔜 = content authored
> unit-by-unit (Step 3). Faith content is optional (faith toggle). Moving one Adventure shifts the
> sequence — it never breaks the curriculum.

> **🕎 Holidays are configurable.** The Ferrell family observes **biblical Feast Days** rather than
> Christmas/Easter traditions, so **no holiday-specific crafts or activities are assumed**. The December
> unit is reframed as *Traditions & Family Celebrations (fully configurable)* — adaptable to each
> family's own celebrations and Feast Days.

**Units:** 13 · **Adventures:** 72 · **Built so far:** 10 / 72

### Academic spine (how skills build)
- **Math:** number sense → addition/subtraction → money & measurement → **fractions** (cooking) → **multiplication/division** → data & graphs → geometry → multi-step word problems & review.
- **English:** greetings & vocabulary → sentences → reading & comprehension → grammar → **creative & descriptive writing** → summaries → **public speaking & presentations**.
- **Languages:** Tagalog **and** Hiligaynon woven through every adventure (greetings → vocabulary → conversation → reading/writing).
- **Bible (optional):** a gentle progression — Creation & Old-Testament heroes (Jul–Oct) → life & parables of Jesus (Nov) → the **Nativity at Christmas** (Dec) → the Great Commission.

## Scheduling & the Teacher Portal (planned)

- **Class days:** Monday, Tuesday, Friday, weekly. Adventure 1 starts **Mon, Jul 6, 2026**.
- **Auto-unlock:** each Adventure unlocks on its scheduled date; the Teacher clicks **Start Adventure** — no manual lesson creation.
- **Flexible:** the Teacher can **unlock early, move, duplicate, skip, or archive** an Adventure; later Adventures simply shift down the calendar (order matters, not the fixed date).
- **Family Portal** shows Today's Adventure, what's next, a countdown, and any shopping list.
