# 🎓 PEDAGOGY — Wonder Journey OS

Wonder Journey OS blends **family learning, project-based learning, storytelling, and
age-appropriate instruction**. This document defines the educational standards **every
lesson must follow.**

---

## 🌟 Mission

> Children should never feel they are attending an online class.
> They should feel like they are **exploring the world with their family.**
> Learning should feel like an **adventure.**

## Learning Principles

Every adventure should develop: **Knowledge · Skills · Character · Creativity ·
Communication · Critical Thinking · Confidence · Family Connection · Curiosity · Gratitude.**

---

## 👧🧒 Age Groups (the Ferrell children)

| Tier | Age | Child(ren) | How they learn best | Max explanation before interaction |
|------|-----|-----------|---------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 🌱 **Explorer** | 7–8 | **Selah** | Play, stories, movement, songs, repetition, pictures | **3–5 min** |
| 🌿 **Adventurer** | 9–10 | **Asa, Ezra** | Research, problem-solving, independent reading/writing, map skills, multiplication, simple presentations | **5–8 min** |
| 🔥 **Trailblazer** | 11–12 | **Rylee** | Leadership, critical thinking, creative writing, presentations, independent research, discussion, **mentoring younger siblings** | **8–10 min** |

**Explorer — avoid:** long paragraphs, complex instructions, abstract concepts, heavy
note-taking. **Use:** short sentences, large text, colorful visuals, hands-on learning.

> These levels power the **"Missions for Every Explorer"** cards in every adventure
> (see `AGE_FRAMEWORK.md`).

## ⏱️ Screen Time (important)

Children should **never** stare at the screen continuously. After **~8–10 minutes**,
break for a real activity: **stand up · move · draw · cook · answer · discuss · play ·
point on a map · find an object · measure something.**

> Every adventure's subject cards include **"Try it"** activities to trigger these breaks —
> keep them, and pause the lesson to do them.

---

## 📚 Level Ladders

**Reading** — Explorer: simple vocabulary, large fonts, minimal text · Adventurer: short
paragraphs · Trailblazer: longer passages, research tasks.

**Math** — Explorer: count, sort, compare, patterns, simple +/− · Adventurer: ×/÷,
fractions, money, measurement · Trailblazer: multi-step problems, graphs, geometry, applied math.

**English** — Explorer: vocabulary, speaking, reading, listening, simple writing ·
Adventurer: paragraphs, grammar, comprehension, spelling, creative writing · Trailblazer:
essays, summaries, presentations, public speaking, research.

**Tagalog** — Explorer: colors, numbers, greetings, family, animals, food · Adventurer:
questions, simple conversations · Trailblazer: reading, storytelling, translation.

**Hiligaynon** — same progression as Tagalog.

## 🙏 Bible Stories

Use language appropriate to each child's age. **Never** use unnecessarily frightening or
graphic descriptions. Emphasize **love, hope, faith, wisdom, forgiveness, courage,
kindness, service, respect, stewardship.** Explain historical events in age-appropriate
language. *(All faith content stays optional behind the faith toggle.)*

## 🔬 Science
**Observation before explanation.** Ask **"What do you notice?"** *before* teaching the concept.

## 🗺️ Geography
**Always teach with maps.** Children should **see** locations, not just memorize names
(World → Asia → Philippines → island group → province → destination).

## 🍳 Cooking
Recipes must be **safe, simple, and age-appropriate**, with **adult supervision** and
**allergy reminders** where relevant.

---

## 💬 Discussions
Every lesson: **Think → Pair (Family) → Share → Reflect.**

## 💛 Positive Education
**Never embarrass a child. Never compare siblings.** Celebrate **effort, curiosity,
kindness, and improvement.**

## 📝 Assessment
Measure **understanding, communication, participation, and application** — not
memorization alone.

**Quizzes** — Explorer: fun, visual, short · Adventurer: interactive, application ·
Trailblazer: reasoning, reflection, leadership.

## ♿ Accessibility
Support **different learning speeds and reading abilities**, and **visual, auditory, and
hands-on** learners.

---

## ✅ Final Rule — before publishing any lesson, ask:

1. Is this **appropriate for a 7-year-old**?
2. Is this **engaging for a 9-year-old**?
3. Is this **challenging for a 12-year-old**?
4. Can the **family enjoy it together**?

**If any answer is "No," revise the lesson.**
