# 🌴 Wonder Journey OS — Design Bible

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Active
- **Priority:** High

> Defines the complete visual identity of Wonder Journey. Every screen, component,
> lesson, illustration, animation, sound, and interaction must follow this Design
> Bible. If any design conflicts with it, redesign it. **The
> [Constitution](../CONSTITUTION.md) always has higher priority.**

---

## Design Philosophy

Wonder Journey is **not software — it is an experience.** Children should never
feel they are using an educational website. They should feel they've opened an
animated children's storybook · an interactive travel journal · an adventure
scrapbook · a passport book · a premium children's museum · a Disney-quality
educational experience.

Every screen should feel **handcrafted · warm · magical · inviting · alive.**

---

## Emotional Goals

Every screen should create: **wonder · joy · curiosity · comfort · safety ·
adventure · family connection · creativity · hope · discovery.**

> The interface should disappear. The adventure should remain.

---

## Design References

**Inspiration:** Disney · Pixar · National Geographic Kids · children's museums ·
travel journals · adventure scrapbooks · premium storybooks · paper craft ·
watercolor illustration · Canva Presentation Mode. *(Recreate the emotional
experience with original assets — never copy copyrighted artwork.)*

**Never imitate:** corporate software · Google Classroom · Moodle · Canvas LMS ·
PowerPoint editor · Notion · business dashboards.

---

## Color System

Colors should feel natural, warm, friendly, child-safe.

- **Primary:** Ocean Blue · Sky Blue · Leaf Green · Palm Green · Golden Yellow ·
  Coral Orange · Sunset Pink · Warm Sand · Paper White · Soft Brown
- **Accent:** Volcano Red · Festival Purple · Mango Yellow · Lagoon Teal · Forest
  Green · Banana Leaf · Rice Gold
- **Neutral:** Warm White · Cream · Light Kraft Paper · Stone Gray

> Never use harsh black backgrounds. Never use corporate gray.

---

## Typography

Extremely readable for children ages **7, 9, 10, 12**.

**Fonts:** Fredoka · Baloo 2 · Nunito · Quicksand · Atkinson Hyperlegible · M PLUS Rounded.

| Element | Size | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Adventure titles | 60–72px | rounded · bold · friendly |
| Scene titles | 34–42px | rounded |
| Body | 24–28px | readable |
| Buttons | 20–24px | bold · rounded |

> Never use corporate typography. Never use dense paragraphs.

---

## Layout & Storybook Rule

Every Adventure behaves like an **illustrated storybook** — never long scrolling
pages. `Scene → Next → Scene → Next → Scene`. Each scene occupies nearly the full
screen and resembles a printed children's picture book, including **illustration ·
story · interaction · question · adventure** — not just text.

**Visual hierarchy (what a child notices first):** 1) Illustration · 2) Title ·
3) Interactive activity · 4) Question · 5) Supporting text.

---

## Paper Style

The lesson area resembles a notebook / travel journal / adventure book / passport:
paper texture · watercolor · scrapbook. Include **washi tape · paper clips ·
passport stamps · compass · decorative borders · stickers · handwritten notes ·
soft shadows · rounded corners.**

---

## Real Educational Media

When teaching real places, always use **real maps, photographs, landmarks, food,
animals, volcanoes, historical sites, cultural artifacts, geography, plants.**
Original illustrations are used only for mascots · decorations · UI · passport ·
badges · storybook elements · fantasy scenes · Bible narrative scenes. **Never
replace authentic educational content with AI-generated artwork.** *(See
[05_REAL_MEDIA_POLICY](../REAL_MEDIA_POLICY.md).)*

---

## Illustration Style

Warm · friendly · watercolor-inspired · soft outlines · expressive · playful ·
timeless. **No generic AI style. No clip art.**

## Animation & Transitions

Animations support learning, never distract — **calm, natural, meaningful:**
clouds · ocean waves · palm leaves · butterflies · birds · fireflies · sparkles ·
cooking steam · compass rotation · passport stamp · treasure chest · badge unlock ·
tree growth · page flip · sunlight · water ripples.

**Transitions** (never abrupt): storybook page turn · passport stamp · journal
flip · cloud wipe · watercolor fade · camera flyover · treasure reveal · sunrise ·
sunset · adventure fade.

---

## Sound Design

Optional, never forced. **Ambient:** ocean · forest · birds · rain · kitchen ·
festival · nature · reflection · adventure theme. **UI:** correct/wrong answer ·
passport stamp · badge unlock · treasure. Always include **mute · volume ·
captions · reduced-motion compatibility.**

---

## Presentation Mode

Adventure Theater behaves like Canva Presentation Mode: fullscreen · one scene ·
one story · one experience · no scrolling. **Teacher controls are hidden from
Family View.**

---

## Components (all reusable)

Adventure Card · Passport Stamp · Badge · Recipe Card · Journal Card · Map Card ·
Speech Bubble · Quiz Card · Character Bubble · Progress Card · Celebration Banner ·
Sticker · Achievement Toast · Timeline Entry.

---

## Accessibility & Responsive

**Accessibility:** keyboard navigation · screen readers · high-contrast mode ·
reduced motion · alt text · readable font sizes · color contrast · touch-friendly
controls.

**Responsive:** desktop · laptop · tablet · large TV · future mobile — with
Teacher View, Family View, and Presentation View each optimized independently.

---

## Quality Checklist

Before approving any screen:

- ✓ Does it feel like Wonder Journey?
- ✓ Would a child smile?
- ✓ Would parents proudly share a screenshot?
- ✓ Does it look like a premium children's book?
- ✓ Does it feel handcrafted?
- ✓ Is it visually calm?
- ✓ Is it educational?
- ✓ Is it joyful?

**If any answer is NO — redesign it.**

---

## Final Design Promise

> Wonder Journey should become one of the most beautiful educational platforms ever
> created. Every page should be worthy of being printed as a children's picture
> book. Every interaction should create wonder. Every Adventure should become a
> treasured childhood memory.
