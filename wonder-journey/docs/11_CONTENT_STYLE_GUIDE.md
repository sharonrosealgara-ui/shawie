# ✍️ Wonder Journey OS — Content Style Guide

- **Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Active · **Priority:** High

> Writing standards for **all** content — lessons, stories, quizzes, recipes,
> dialogue, journal prompts, teacher notes, parent summaries, Bible lessons,
> activities. Wonder Journey should never read like a textbook — it reads like a
> **premium interactive children's storybook.** Governed by the
> [Constitution](../CONSTITUTION.md); voice/visuals pair with the
> [Design Bible](01_DESIGN_BIBLE.md).

---

## Audience & philosophy
Written for the family — Rylee (12) · Ezra (10) · Asa (9) · Selah (7) — learning
**together**, with optional extensions for older kids. Children learn by
**discovering, exploring, touching, building, cooking, creating, playing,
wondering, reflecting, and teaching someone else** — never memorization alone.
Every lesson is an experience.

## Voice
**Warm · friendly · encouraging · joyful · hopeful · curious · respectful ·
conversational · natural.** Never corporate, formal, cold, robotic, academic, or
overly childish. No unnecessary jargon.

## Storytelling
Every lesson **begins with a story** that introduces the objective naturally.
> ❌ "Today we will learn about volcanoes."
> ✅ "Today, our guide has spotted smoke rising from a mysterious mountain. Could it be a volcano? Let's investigate together!"

## Sentence style
Short sentences · simple vocabulary · active voice · positive language · questions ·
examples. Never long paragraphs — break into small sections.

## Age bands
- **7–8:** very short sentences · big illustrations · games · movement · songs · matching · drawing.
- **9–10:** simple explanations · hands-on projects · discovery · observation · maps · experiments.
- **11–12:** discussion · critical thinking · research · creative writing · leadership.
Every lesson includes optional **extension challenges** for older children.

## Family learning
Every lesson offers ways to **talk · pray · cook · build · draw · reflect ·
celebrate — together.** Parents are never observers.

## Subject integration
- **Language (Tagalog/Hiligaynon):** teach naturally in context, not vocab lists.
  ❌ "Apple = Mansanas" ✅ "Let's buy some *mansanas* at the market."
- **English:** stories, reading, conversation, writing, journals, role play; vocabulary in context.
- **Math:** never separate — cooking, maps, shopping, travel, measuring, building (times tables, recipe measurements, distances, counting islands, market budgeting).
- **Science:** discovery — observe · experiment · predict · test · discuss; connect to the real world.
- **History:** time travel — timelines, maps, artifacts, stories, real photos, dioramas (great for Ezra & Asa).
- **Geography:** always real maps/photos/satellite + interactive exploration; never text alone.
- **Cooking:** real photos · ingredients · measurements · conversions · safety · nutrition · Tagalog/Hiligaynon vocab · math + science · family challenge · reflection · recipe card · cookbook entry.
- **Art:** encourage creativity, never require perfection; offer options per child's interests.

## Bible
History of Jesus · Bible geography/timeline · parables · miracles · disciples ·
prayer · reflection · character · gratitude · service · hope · respect. **Avoid
assuming Christmas/Easter**; respect the family's biblical **Feast Days**. Keep it
age-appropriate · hopeful · respectful · accurate · non-graphic.

## Values (emerge through stories, not lectures)
Respect · kindness · responsibility · gratitude · hospitality · helping others ·
generosity · faith · forgiveness · patience · love.

## Personalization (every adventure)
Optional activities for **🎨 Rylee** (animals/nature/crafts/embroidery/knitting/design),
**🔨 Ezra** (history/building/LEGO/miniatures/woodworking/cooking), **⚙️ Asa**
(engineering/mechanics/outdoor/animals/experiments/building), **🏘️ Selah**
(drawing/miniature towns/cooking/baking/art/creative play). No child left out.

## Quizzes (feel like games, never exams)
Multiple choice · matching · picture ID · map challenges · treasure hunts · drag &
drop · sorting · crosswords · word search · memory · speaking prompts · family
discussion. Adaptive difficulty.

## Reflections (end every lesson)
"What surprised you today?" · "Your favorite discovery?" · "What are you thankful to
God for?" · "A new Tagalog/Hiligaynon word you learned?" · "How can today's lesson
help others?"

## Family challenge (one per lesson)
Teach Mom a new Tagalog word · cook together · build a volcano · draw today's animal
· find the place on a map · practice vocab at dinner · read a Bible passage · take a
family photo.

## Parent summary (generated after each lesson)
Overview · vocabulary learned · academic skills · Bible connection · cooking/crafts
completed · photos · quiz results · next-lesson preview · suggested family activity.

## Accessibility
Readable fonts · alt text · captions · clear language · reduced-motion · color
contrast · screen readers.

## Final quality check (rewrite if any answer is NO)
✓ Appropriate for ages 7–12? ✓ Supports Shaun & Taylor's goals? ✓ Family
participation? ✓ Warm, encouraging writing? ✓ Visually engaging? ✓ Teaches through
exploration? ✓ Real educational media where appropriate? ✓ Encourages Christian
character? ✓ Would the children look forward to the next lesson?

> **Content promise:** children should remember the *experience* long after they
> forget individual facts. The goal is joyful, meaningful memories through learning
> together.
