## Near-term priorities (post-session)

   interactive content for Units 3–13 (62 adventures), one unit at a time to
   preserve quality.
2. **Source real media (Phase 8 assets)** — editorial: download/license photos &
   maps into `assets/`, fill credit/license, flip `status:"ready"`.
3. **Six-mascot cast (Design Bible)** — migrate from single guide to Tala/Lila/
   Kiko/Isla/Agila/Coco.
4. **Adventure Builder UI (Phase 10)** — a form to author adventures without code.
5. **SaaS foundation (Phase 19–20)** — Supabase accounts, PWA, Make.com summaries.
