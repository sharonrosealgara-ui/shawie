## World 1 — Discover the Philippines
- **Schedule:** Mondays, Tuesdays, Fridays · **2026-07-06 → 2026-12-18**
- **72 Adventures · 13 Units** — one continuous story, July → December.

## Model
`World → Unit → Adventure`. Every adventure carries metadata used by the map,
Teacher Portal, and Parent Dashboard:

- `title · unit · week · date · day`
- `objective` — the day's learning goal
- `value` — the character/faith value
- `math` · `english` · `bible` — woven subject focuses
- `built` — whether full interactive content exists yet

## Authoring principle
Build the **framework first** (schedule + metadata for all 72 — done), then author
full interactive content **one unit at a time** so quality never suffers. Units 1–2
(a1–a10) are fully authored; Units 3–13 are scheduled with metadata and are usable
in planning tools today.

## Every adventure aims to include
Story · exploration · real maps · real photography · language · math · science ·
culture · Bible · hands-on activity · quiz · reflection · celebration · family
challenge · passport stamp · badge · memory. *(Experience Standards, Constitution.)*

## Faith integration
Each adventure has a Bible focus (creation → journeys → feasts → Great Commission),
kept hopeful, age-appropriate, non-graphic, and **Feast-Day-respectful** (no assumed
Christmas/Easter; December is configurable).
