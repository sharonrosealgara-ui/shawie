# ✍️ Wonder Journey OS — Storytelling & UI Copywriting Directive

- **Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Active · Pairs with [11_CONTENT_STYLE_GUIDE](11_CONTENT_STYLE_GUIDE.md) (lesson prose) — this doc governs **interface copy**

> **The owner's north star — every word must serve this sentence:**
> *"Every lesson, message, and interaction should strengthen family relationships,
> encourage curiosity, celebrate God's creation, respect the family's Christian
> values and educational preferences, and make learning feel like an unforgettable
> shared adventure rather than a classroom task."*

> Wonder Journey should never *sound* like software. Every label, button, empty
> state, and message reads like a page from a beautiful adventure book — warm,
> encouraging, magical, premium — while staying clear for children and adults.
> **Layout, navigation, and features never change under this directive; only words.**

## Voice
A trusted teacher · a caring guide · an exciting travel companion · a warm family
friend. Never robotic, corporate, or generic. One consistent voice everywhere.

## Banned vocabulary → storybook replacements
Dashboard/Manage/Statistics/Administration → journey, adventure, memories ·
"Continue/Open/Click here" → *✈️ Continue Our Journey, 🌟 Discover Something New* ·
"Completed" → *another chapter written* · "No data available" → *"Our adventure
book is waiting for its very first memory."* Errors are kind and never blame:
*"It looks like our adventure hit a small bump — let's try that again together."*

## Copy pass v1 ✅ (v0.29.1 — shipped)
- **Home hero:** "Your backpacks are packed and your passports are ready…" +
  **🌴 Begin Today's Adventure / ✈️ Continue Our Journey / 🧭 Explore the
  Adventure Map.**
- **Stats:** Places Explored · Explorer Points · Passport Stamps · Adventure
  Achievements. **Up Next → "Today's Journey Begins Here."**
- **Quizzes:** "Adventure Challenge" (kicker + scene label); warm mascot feedback
  retained ("Yes! That's right! ⭐ / Good try! Let's learn it together 💛").
- **Celebrations:** 🧭 Back to Our Map · "Stay a little longer 🌺"; World-1-done
  message celebrates the *family of explorers*.
- **Empty states:** timeline & cookbook now invite instead of report; passport
  intro reads as a family journey.
- Already on-voice from earlier work: toasts, birthday/milestone modals, Cooking
  Academy ("We Cooked It!"), Lesson Builder guidance, mascot lines.

## Continuing program 🔜
Sweep remaining surfaces one by one (map, badges, tree, blessings, settings,
teacher portal helper text, loading/error moments) against the checklist:
✓ warm ✓ clear at age 7 ✓ short buttons ✓ consistent voice ✓ accessible ✓ never
repetitive praise. Every new feature ships with storybook copy from day one.
