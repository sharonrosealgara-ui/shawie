# 🎨 Wonder Journey OS — Brand Guidelines

- **Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Active · **Priority:** High

> The complete Wonder Journey brand identity. Everything the family sees, hears, and
> experiences should reflect **one consistent brand** — not just software, but a
> memorable family adventure. Pairs with the [Design Bible](01_DESIGN_BIBLE.md);
> the [Constitution](../CONSTITUTION.md) always wins.

---

## Mission
Inspire families to discover the beauty of God's creation, Filipino culture,
learning, and family life through joyful adventures.

## Personality
Warm · joyful · curious · creative · hopeful · adventurous · respectful ·
faith-centered · family-oriented · encouraging. **Never** corporate · cold ·
clinical · overly formal · overstimulating.

## Promise
Every adventure creates memories · every lesson strengthens family relationships ·
every interaction encourages curiosity.

## Audience
**Primary:** Shaun's family. **Future:** Christian homeschooling families · families
wanting cultural education · teachers · children 7–12 · parents · grandparents.

## Logo
Communicates adventure · learning · family · discovery · nature · travel ·
storytelling. Must work in light/dark mode · print · presentation mode ·
certificates · passport · favicons · social. *(Current mark: 🌏 globe app icon.)*

## Color palette
- **Primary:** Ocean Blue · Sky Blue · Leaf Green · Warm Sand · Sunshine Yellow · Coral Orange · Soft White.
- **Accent:** Volcano Red · Festival Purple · Rice Gold · Lagoon Teal · Forest Green.
- Use color intentionally to support emotion. Never harsh black/corporate gray.

## Typography
Fredoka · Baloo 2 · Nunito · Quicksand · Atkinson Hyperlegible — always large,
rounded, readable, friendly, child-appropriate.

## Visual style
Inspired by storybooks · travel journals · scrapbooks · nature · watercolor · paper
crafts · interactive museums · Disney-quality storytelling. Use rounded shapes ·
paper textures · illustrated borders · soft shadows · friendly spacing.

## Photography
Educational content uses **real** photos · maps · landmarks · food · wildlife ·
festivals · cultural artifacts · locations. Illustrations only for mascots · UI ·
decoration · fantasy · Bible narrative scenes.

## Mascots
🐢 **Tala** (Travel Guide) · 🦜 **Lila** (Language Explorer) · 🐒 **Kiko** (Adventure
Leader) · 🌺 **Isla** (Nature Guide) · 🦅 **Agila** (Wildlife Expert) · 🍳 **Coco**
(Cooking Buddy). Appear consistently platform-wide. *(Current build ships one guide,
"Sinag"; six-mascot migration tracked in [08_DECISIONS](08_DECISIONS.md).)*

## Iconography
Rounded · simple · friendly · consistent · handcrafted. Avoid generic corporate icon
packs where possible.

## Tone of voice
Write as if speaking to a family gathered around a table — encouragement · curiosity
· wonder · respect · warmth. Avoid pressure · fear · negative language · shaming.

## Motion & sound
Motion: gentle · purposeful · natural · calming — never overwhelming. Sound: optional
ambient (ocean · forest · birds · cooking · rain · festival · adventure · reflection)
with mute always available.

## Accessibility
Readable text · clear contrast · alt text · captions · keyboard support · reduced
motion · large touch targets.

## Brand experience
Families should describe Wonder Journey as **beautiful · peaceful · memorable ·
interactive · creative · educational · faith-filled · family-centered · magical.**

---

## Brand quality check (redesign if any NO)
✓ Feels like Wonder Journey? ✓ Would a child smile? ✓ Would parents proudly
recommend it? ✓ Visually beautiful? ✓ Welcoming? ✓ Strengthens the family experience?

> Wonder Journey is more than a platform — it is a family's journey through learning,
> faith, culture, creativity, and adventure. Every interaction should remind the
> family they are **creating memories together.**
