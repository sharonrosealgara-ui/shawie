# 🎯 Age-Appropriate Learning Framework — Wonder Journey OS

Wonder Journey is built for **siblings of different ages learning together**. The
curriculum never assumes every child is at the same level. **The family experiences one
shared adventure, but each child receives age-appropriate tasks.**

> **In the app today `✅`:** every adventure shows a **"Missions for Every Explorer"**
> section with three level cards — Explorer, Adventurer, Trailblazer — so each child does
> the tasks that fit them, and older kids help the younger ones.

---

## The Three Levels

| Tier | Age | Focus |
|------|-----|-------|
| 🌱 **Explorer** | ~7–8 | Pictures, listening, tracing, matching, counting, simple reading/writing, drawing, movement, hands-on, lots of encouragement, minimal text |
| 🌿 **Adventurer** | ~9–10 | Reading paragraphs, independent writing, vocabulary, multiplication/division/fractions, problem solving, research, maps, guided cooking, creative thinking |
| 🔥 **Trailblazer** | ~11–12 | Critical thinking, paragraph writing, presentations, leadership, independent research, complex math, longer reading, discussion, reflection, **mentoring younger siblings** |

Every adventure aligns all three levels to the **same objective**, varying only the difficulty.

---

## How each subject differentiates

**English** — Explorer: sight words, short sentences, speaking · Adventurer: reading,
grammar, sentence & paragraph work · Trailblazer: creative writing, summaries,
presentations, opinion writing, public speaking.

**Mathematics** — Explorer: counting, +/−, shapes, patterns, time, money · Adventurer:
×/÷, fractions, measurement, word problems · Trailblazer: advanced fractions, intro
decimals, multi-step problems, geometry, graphs, real-life applications.

**Tagalog & Hiligaynon** — Explorer: greetings, colors, numbers, animals, food, songs ·
Adventurer: sentence building, questions, role play, vocabulary · Trailblazer:
conversation, storytelling, reading, writing, translation.

**Science** — Explorer: observe, touch, draw, identify · Adventurer: compare, classify,
explain · Trailblazer: investigate, experiment, research, present findings.

**Bible stories** — Explorer: simple storytelling, pictures, songs, one key lesson ·
Adventurer: discussion, character lessons, application · Trailblazer: age-appropriate
historical context, Bible geography, connections, reflection, leadership.
**Never frightening or graphic** — focus on hope, wisdom, kindness, courage, faith,
forgiveness, and God's love.

**Cooking** — Explorer: safe mixing, pouring, measuring with help, decorating ·
Adventurer: read recipes, prep, measure, follow steps · Trailblazer: lead part of the
recipe, explain measurements, help younger siblings, kitchen responsibility.

**Maps** — Explorer: point, identify, color, match · Adventurer: locate, compare,
measure, directions · Trailblazer: analyze, travel-plan, distances, research destinations.

**Quizzes** — Explorer: picture questions, matching, true/false · Adventurer: multiple
choice, short answer, map activities · Trailblazer: critical thinking, application,
discussion, creative response. *(Today's built-in quiz is one shared set; the Missions
cards carry the leveled extension tasks. Fuller per-level quiz banks are planned `🔜`.)*

## Character Development

Keep values concrete and age-appropriate — teach through **stories, role-play, family
discussion, and practical actions**, not abstract concepts without examples.

## Family Learning

Activities differ in difficulty, but the **family completes the adventure together**.
Older children help younger siblings. **Encourage teamwork, not competition.** Celebrate
every child's progress.

---

## Content Review Checklist (before finalizing any adventure)

✔ Age appropriateness · ✔ Reading level · ✔ Vocabulary difficulty · ✔ Math difficulty ·
✔ Emotional maturity · ✔ Cultural accuracy · ✔ Bible accuracy · ✔ Safety ·
✔ Accessibility · ✔ Family engagement.

If content is too hard or too easy for a tier, adjust it.

---

## Final Principle

> Children should leave every adventure feeling **successful, encouraged, and excited**
> for the next lesson. **No child left behind. No child bored.** Every child challenged at
> the right level, sharing one unforgettable family adventure.
