Go Live

Two ways to publish it.

Once your invitation looks perfect, share it as a real link. Pick whichever route fits you — both are free.

Easiest

Publish as a Canva Website

Canva hosts it for you and gives you a free link. Republish any time and the live page updates instantly.

  1. Click Share → Publish as website.
  2. Choose the free canva.site domain and type your names.
  3. Set navigation to Scroll for a one-page invite, then Publish.
  4. Copy your link — it's ready to send.
Your own link

Host it free on Netlify

Want a branded web address? Export your design and drop it onto Netlify's free tier for your own custom URL.

  1. In Canva, Download your pages as images (or export the site files).
  2. Go to netlify.com/drop and drag your folder in.
  3. Netlify publishes instantly on a free link you can rename.
  4. Upgrade later to connect a custom domain like yourname.com.
Send It Out

Share your invitation.

One link. Every guest.

Your digital invitation lives at a single link — no printing, no postage, and you can update details right up to the big day.

  • Text or email the link, or share it in your family group chat.
  • Turn the link into a QR code (Canva makes one for you) and print it on your save-the-dates.
  • Guests tap through to RSVP, see the schedule, and find directions — all from their phone.
  • Need a change? Edit in Canva, republish, and everyone sees the update.
